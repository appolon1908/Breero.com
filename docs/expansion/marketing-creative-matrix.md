# BREERO — 35-service visual campaign system
**Status:** Creative directions, not ads launched. **Markets:** US, Mexico, Spain, Dominican Republic. **Company:** BREERO only; not Codestra, not CHIQUIJUGO.

## Three distinct campaign audiences
1. **B2C homeowner/customer:** real home problem → trusted service process → request a callback / check coverage.
2. **B2B commercial service buyer:** show interruptions to business operations → maintenance coordination → request a commercial consultation.
3. **B2B service provider (BREERO Pro):** show a real trade-business owner, staff, tools, and organized opportunity handling → apply to join the marketplace. Recruitment imagery should not imply guaranteed jobs/revenue/lead volume.

## Creative patterns
- **Diagnostic / problem:** credible professional inspecting issue in a real home/business, useful for high-intent paid ads.
- **Solution / result:** clear finished work or permitted real before-and-after images; no fabricated results.
- **Trust / process:** technician explaining the issue or company owner discussing scope and approvals.
- **Urgency / seasonal:** AC repair in hot climates, heating/chimneys in cold regions, flooding/backup power in storm-prone periods; don't dramatize danger.
- **Recurring care:** landscaping, cleaning, pool, pest control and maintenance routes.
- **Provider recruitment:** owner + small team + field kit/van + clear CTA to apply; show realistic marketplace benefits.
- **Location identity:** authentic properties, climate, trade equipment and local language—not generic flag overlays or invented local providers.

## Short copy examples
- B2C English: “AC not cooling? Request a callback.” / “Need a plumber? Check your local options.”
- B2C Español: “¿Tu aire no enfría? Solicita una llamada.” / “¿Necesitas un plomero? Consulta cobertura.”
- Commercial buyer: “Facilities need maintenance? Request a service consultation.”
- Commercial Spanish: “¿Tu negocio necesita mantenimiento? Solicita asesoría.”
- Provider English: “Run an HVAC business? Apply to BREERO Pro.”
- Provider Español: “¿Tienes una empresa de climatización? Solicita unirte a BREERO Pro.”
- Never say “7 pros available”, “instant verified booking”, “guaranteed leads”, “same-day technician”, or “licensed & insured” without jurisdiction-specific evidence.

## Asset production
- Meta/Facebook/Instagram feed: **1080 × 1350 (4:5)**, plus **1080 × 1080 (1:1)**.
- Stories/Reels: **1080 × 1920 (9:16)**; keep logos, legal text and CTA away from edge UI overlays.
- Google responsive display: separate **1200 × 628 (1.91:1)**, **1200 × 1200 (1:1)**; clean product/service photography with **no overlaid promotional text or brand mark** in the photo asset. Google logo uploaded as a separate permitted logo asset. Reference: Google Ads image formats and responsive display specs.
- Optional landing hero: **1600 × 900 (16:9)** plus mobile crop; mobile-first layout, alt text and optimized WebP where supported.
- 35 services × 3 personas × 3 social-media crops = **315 core social assets**, before language/location variants. Avoid automatically multiplying every city into identical low-quality graphics; prioritize active markets and review ROI.
- Each asset: service slug, audience, country, region/city target (if relevant), language, scenario, image model/release provenance, source, final copy, landing URL, consent review, and performance tracking.

## Brand & localization
- Keep BREERO orange (#FF8500), navy (#10213B), blue (#009FE8); place official BREERO logo consistently in social creatives. Don't put logo where Google image-asset policy disallows it.
- **US**: English base, Spanish variants only where actually useful; hot climate and winter ads adapt by market.
- **Mexico**: es-MX vocabulary (“plomería”, “aire acondicionado”), local architecture and trades.
- **Spain**: es-ES vocabulary (“fontanería”, “climatización”), locally plausible buildings and uniforms.
- **Dominican Republic**: es-DO (“plomería”, “aires”, “inversores” where appropriate), realistic homes and commercial sites.
- Language and legal opt-in cannot be inferred from national origin. Respect privacy, copyright, portrait releases, property releases, and trade-specific safety claims.

## Funnel and tracking
- Keep B2C homeowner / B2B commercial buyer / B2B service-provider segments separate in BREERO HighLevel.
- Meta campaign labels, UTM terms, and landing content must align with each audience and actual country.
- B2C and B2B buyer CTA: callback/request information only where serviceability is not verified; provider CTA: apply to BREERO Pro if an application flow is live and approved.
- Don't run paid ads for unverified city-service pairs; don't turn public contractor contact data into marketing opt-in.
- Test 2–3 hooks per service and locale before scaling; optimize on source-verified qualified request or approved application, not views alone.
